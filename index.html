A ship that shouldn't exist.

  1. −340m

    The sonar returned something solid

    On a marine research expedition off the Malabar coast, Shiva's team picked up an outline that had no business being there — a ship, fully intact, resting where nothing survives a century. It was The Leviathan, lost to the deep long before anyone kept records.

  2. −361m

    The hold was still breathing

    Half-buried in coral and silence, a crystal pulsed faint blue: the Aqua Core. The moment his hand closed around it the hull convulsed, the way a body does when it wakes, and the ocean pulled him under.

  3. −0m

    He surfaced four days later

    Changed. His body could move like water, hold its shape like steel, and vanish like mist. The wreck had never truly died — it had been waiting for a captain worth waking for.

The crystal didn't kill him. It rewrote him.

What the Aqua Core gave him

Hydrokinesis

Full command over water in any form — from open ocean to a single drop of dew — bent to his will on contact or from a distance.

Tide forge

Condenses seawater into blades, spears and barriers hard enough to stop a strike, then releases them back into mist a moment later.

Current sense

Reads vibration and pressure moving through water to find danger, distress, or a lie forming underneath calm words.

Healing mist

A fine, glowing sea-mist drawn from the Core that closes wounds and eases pain for anyone caught in the crossfire.

Guarding the sea that remade him

His mission

Leviathan protects people from what the ocean hides — smugglers using its silence, disasters building beneath its surface, and everything it swallows without warning. He answers calls the coast guard can't reach in time, and problems no one else believes are real.

The man inside the armour

Quiet, watchful, unhurried. He spent his life studying the sea before it changed him and still respects it more than he fears it. He listens all the way to the end before he acts, and never makes a promise he isn't certain the tide will let him keep.
